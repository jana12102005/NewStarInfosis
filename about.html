Origin Story

FROM VISIONARY ROOTS TO ENTERPRISE SCALE

Established in 2024, New Star Infosis emerged to address a widespread fragmentation in technology: mainstream software agencies lacked deep computational science acumen, while academic research institutes lacked commercial software deployment velocity.

New Star Infosis bridges this divide. Bringing together senior artificial intelligence architects, biological data engineers, and cloud infrastructure specialists, we create software that scales seamlessly from laboratory compute nodes to global cloud platforms.
